Why is Steganography Important?

Answer: Steganography is the practice of hiding secret information inside an ordinary file, such as an image, audio file, or video, so that the existence of the hidden information is not obvious. Unlike encryption, which makes information unreadable without a key, steganography focuses on concealing the information itself.

In digital forensics, steganography matters because criminals may use it to secretly hide evidence such as documents, messages, or other data within innocent-looking files. Forensic investigators therefore need to identify, extract, and verify hidden data while preserving the integrity of the original evidence.
